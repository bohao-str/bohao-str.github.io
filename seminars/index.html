Seminars

These reading seminars are organized by students at Peking University, including Yutong Huang, Yihang Yu, and Bohao Zhuang.

All times are Beijing time (UTC+8).

Basic Concepts of String Theory

Time: Fridays, 19:20–21:20
Venue: 物理学院南408

A reading seminar following Ralph Blumenhagen, Dieter Lüst, and Stefan Theisen, Basic Concepts of String Theory.

The book develops classical and quantum string theory, with topics including conformal field theory, superstrings, compactification, and string dualities.

Hori–Vafa: Mirror Symmetry

Time: Saturdays, 10:10–12:00
Venue: 理教111

A reading seminar on Kentaro Hori and Cumrun Vafa, Mirror Symmetry (arXiv:hep-th/0002222).

The paper studies mirror symmetry through supersymmetric sigma models, relating gauged linear sigma models to Landau–Ginzburg theories using T-duality and vortex-generated superpotentials.
