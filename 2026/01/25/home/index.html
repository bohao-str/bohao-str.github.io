About me

I am an undergraduate student at Peking University, majoring in Physics, and will begin my PhD studies in 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Yinan Wang.

My research interests lie at the intersection of string theory and algebraic geometry, with a particular focus on mirror symmetry.

Currently, I am studying special geometry and its applications in string theory, including topics related to flux vacua and effective superpotentials. I am also learning gauge theory and topological quantum field theory from both mathematical and physical perspectives.

This website serves as a personal academic homepage, where I collect notes, blog posts, and materials from my study and research.
