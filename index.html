About Me

I am an undergraduate student in physics at Peking University and will begin my PhD studies under the supervision of Prof. Yinan Wang in September 2026.

My current research concerns the geometric engineering of four-dimensional N=2 superconformal field theories from Type IIA string theory on singular Calabi–Yau threefolds. In particular, I am interested in understanding how physical data of the resulting theories can be extracted from the underlying geometry.

More broadly, my interests include string theory, mirror symmetry, algebraic geometry, and number theory.

I use this website to share notes and materials related to my studies and research.
