About me

I am a first-year PhD student in physics at Peking University, under the supervision of Prof. Yinan Wang.

My current research focuses on the geometric engineering of four-dimensional N = 2 superconformal field theories in Type IIA string theory on singular Calabi–Yau threefolds. In particular, I am interested in understanding how the physical data of these theories can be extracted from the underlying geometry. I am also interested in five-dimensional superconformal field theories engineered in M-theory.

More broadly, my interests include string theory, mirror symmetry, algebraic geometry, and number theory.

I use this website to share notes and materials related to my studies and research.
